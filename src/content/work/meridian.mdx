## The problem

Investing apps tell you what your money is worth, not whether you're getting anywhere. Goal-based investing fixes that: name what you're saving for, pick a portfolio, and a monthly deposit invests toward it.

The hard part isn't the happy path. It's awkward numbers and a slow network, which is where trust is won or lost, as I learned building [analytics at Mailchimp](/work/mailchimp-audience-analytics/).

## What I built

**One screen, three answers:** what it's all worth, how each goal is tracking ("On track for Oct 2028"), and what happened. Scrub the chart and the headline shows that month.

{/* ![Dark mode home screen …](../../assets/work/meridian-dark.png) */}

**Goals you can act on.** Add money or edit a goal and see the payoff first: "This moves House deposit from Oct 2028 to Sep 2028."

{/* ![The goal drawer, adding money …](../../assets/work/meridian-goal.png) */}

**A mock API with real-world mess.** Validation, 404s, 409s and 350 ms of latency, with deliberately awkward data: zeros instead of blanks, a 65-character name, a closed portfolio.

## Decisions that matter

- **Writes finish when the page is right.** A drawer closes only once the changed data has refetched, and writes never retry on their own.
- **Errors land where they belong.** Field messages under the field, a way back from a closed portfolio, and nothing lost on a dropped connection. Three forms, one path.
- **Honest numbers.** A goal that hasn't started says "Starts Dec 1", not $0 and 0%.
- **Color means one thing.** Green and red are gain and loss; goal colors are identity only.

## Accessible by default

Labelled controls and tests that query by role, a chart with a screen-reader summary and arrow-key scrubbing, focus-trapping drawers, and no motion for people who prefer less.

## How it changed

It began as a timeboxed exercise on a private-markets API. That needed a glossary, so I rebuilt it around goals with my own API, and most of the engineering carried over. I paired with AI throughout and stayed the one deciding; the [write-up](https://github.com/LBakerDev/meridian-dashboard/blob/main/docs/how-it-was-built.md) covers what I kept and overrode.
